How ToolsQuark Builds Trust

Every tool is designed to make its purpose, method, limits, and data handling easy to inspect before you rely on a result.

Methods And Sources

Calculators disclose formulas, assumptions, examples, limitations, and references. Original self-checks disclose scoring and validation status. Read the Editorial & Methodology Policy.

Private By Design

Core calculations and assessment answers stay in your browser. Homepage discovery events never include search terms, answers, or calculator inputs. Read the Privacy Policy.

Review And Corrections

Pages include update dates and correction guidance. Learn who maintains the library on the About page or report a reproducible issue through GitHub.
