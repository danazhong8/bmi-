What This Calculator Estimates

This steps-to-distance calculator estimates how many miles or kilometers a step count may represent. It is useful for interpreting phone or wearable step totals when route distance is unknown.

Formula Used

Distance in meters = steps x stride length in meters

The calculator converts meters to miles or kilometers after the stride calculation.

The same step count can produce a different distance when stride length changes, which is why custom stride is useful.

When To Use A Custom Stride

Use a custom stride if you measured a known route and counted steps. Custom stride is better than a general preset when you use the result for repeated route planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is 10,000 steps?

With the default 0.76 m stride, 10,000 steps is about 4.7 miles or 7.6 kilometers. Your actual distance depends on stride length, speed, terrain, and turns.

How far is 5,000 steps?

With the default 0.76 m stride, 5,000 steps is about 2.4 miles or 3.8 kilometers.

How many kilometers is 8,000 steps?

With the default 0.76 m stride, 8,000 steps is about 6.1 kilometers, or about 3.8 miles.

How do I convert steps to miles?

Multiply steps by stride length in meters, then divide by 1,609.344. This calculator does that conversion and rounds the result.

Is this accurate for running?

It is intended for walking estimates. Running stride length can be very different, so a GPS watch or measured route is usually better for running distance.
